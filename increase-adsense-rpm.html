What is AdSense RPM? (And Why You Should Care)

RPM stands for Revenue Per Mille (thousand impressions). It’s the most important metric for AdSense publishers because it measures how much you earn per 1,000 pageviews – regardless of how many ads are clicked. Formula: (Estimated earnings / Pageviews) × 1000.

Why does RPM matter more than CPC or CTR? Because it’s the final outcome. You can have high CPC but low CTR, or high CTR but low CPC. RPM bundles everything into a single number that tells you if your overall monetization is improving. If you double your RPM, you double your revenue with the same traffic – that’s the leverage we want.

Why Is Your AdSense RPM Stuck? (8 Silent Killers)

Before fixing RPM, we need to diagnose. Here are the most common culprits I see in low‑RPM sites:

  • 1. Slow hosting (TTFB > 700ms): The #1 hidden RPM killer.
  • 2. Poor Core Web Vitals: LCP > 2.5s, CLS > 0.1 → ad auction downgrade.
  • 3. Low‑value traffic geography: Too much Tier‑3 traffic.
  • 4. Informational‑only content: No “best”, “vs”, or “review” keywords.
  • 5. Bad ad placement: Ads below the fold, no sticky units.
  • 6. Too many ads: Ad blindness and policy violations.
  • 7. Blocking high‑CPC categories: Overly restrictive ad settings.
  • 8. No mobile optimization: 70%+ traffic is mobile – if your site isn’t smooth, mobile RPM tanks.
